From the game's in-game glossary.

Guns, swords, jetpacks, dashpacks, wands, shape shifting orbs and magic scrolls need a certain level of one stat to equip. Armor needs no stats; it uses the Security Level skill instead, which goes up as you complete important quests for your race's leader.

HOW STATS GROW
You raise a stat by attacking enemies with a weapon that needs that stat: hit enemies with a sword, which needs Strength, until your Strength goes up.
The enemy's level doesn't matter: a level 18 Datro gives the same stat growth as a level 1 Flob. What matters is your own stat and the weapon's requirement. Attacking at Strength 10 with a sword that needs Strength 10 is the fastest growth you can get; at Strength 100, a weapon that needs 100 is. Growth slows as you get closer to 250, so 200 to 201 takes many more attacks than 10 to 11. It's generally best to attack with your highest level weapon, since it has the highest requirement.
Amulets, emblems, badges, gidions or rings that raise your stats can slow your growth, because growth counts your current stat including what equipment adds.
You can also buy a Stat Training Kit from any general store, then buy tokens that turn credits into stat growth. Each use of the kit counts as one attack on an enemy.

STRENGTH
Strength directly increases your health: every point is 1 extra HP. It matters for melee builds, which need the health when fighting up close. It grows by attacking with melee weapons, by smithing, or with a Stat Training Kit. Strength is the main stat for the Bloblic. The cap is 250.
Skills: Melee, Smithing, Reveal. Equipment: swords, dashpacks.

DEXTERITY
Dexterity stands for movement speed and ranged attacks. It grows by attacking with guns, by engineering, or with a Stat Training Kit. Dexterity is the main stat for Humans. The cap is 250.
Skills: Engineering, Mind Control, Pilot, Weapon Tech. Equipment: guns, jetpacks, vehicles.

WISDOM
Wisdom stands for magic and elemental power; exploiting elemental weaknesses mostly depends on it. It grows by attacking with magic or wands, by alchemy, or with a Stat Training Kit. Wisdom is the main stat for the Amphibious. The cap is 250.
Skills: Alchemy, Familiars, Hiding, Magic, Shape Shifting. Equipment: familiar scrolls, magic scrolls, shape shifting orbs, wands.

RESISTANCE
Every species on IIA resists each kind of elemental damage by some amount, and being strong in one usually makes you weak in another. You can see your resistances at the bottom right of the HUD: each number is the percent of that element's damage you resist. The cap for players and monsters is 80, for 80% less damage. See Elements for what each element is weak to.
