From the game's in-game glossary.

Skills are needed to equip weapons, cast spells, craft items and use other abilities.

HOW SKILLS GROW
You level a skill by doing the thing it covers: crafting an item for Alchemy, or attacking with a gun for Weapon Tech. The higher level the action, the more experience it gives, so crafting the highest level item you can is the fastest way to level a crafting skill. Each level needs more experience than the last.
For combat skills, your weapon's level doesn't matter, and nor does the damage you do. What matters is the enemy's level and your own skill level: generally, the higher the enemy, the more experience. The exception is bosses, which give much less experience than normal monsters, and quest monsters, which give nearly none.
You get experience for every hit you land, not for the kill, so a faster weapon levels you faster. A lower level weapon can also keep an enemy alive longer if you want to get as much experience out of it as you can. Non-combat skills like Hiding or Familiars level by doing them over and over.
Once you reach level 10 in any skill you lose protected status and start dropping your inventory when you die. Only special non-drop equipment stays with you. You have thirty minutes to loot your corpse before it vanishes.

WEAPON TECH
Decides the level of gun you can use. The level in a gun's name, like (Lv5), is the Weapon Tech level it needs. Bloblic and Humans use this skill; the Amphibious use Mind Control for their guns.

MIND CONTROL
Decides the level of gun an Amphibious can use. The Amphibious snatch eggs from IIA's native species; once hatched, the newborn beast is given to an Amphibious as a weapon. These newborns are kept from growing, in a sleep-like state, and fire their attacks when the wielder sends them mental signals, much like a gun. The level in a gun's name is the Mind Control level it needs. Bloblic and Humans use Weapon Tech instead.

MELEE
The simple but effective way of stabbing your enemy to death. Its main stat is Strength, and melee fighters want Strength as high as it goes for the extra HP. Dashpacks are Strength-based jetpacks that help you close or open distance on the ground. A melee weapon gives extra resistance while you're holding it, depending on its level. The larger the species, the longer its melee reach.

MAGIC
The easiest and most reliable way to deal elemental damage. Magic depends heavily on Wisdom: wands and magic scrolls both need some. Wands are held like a gun and cast spells with no ammo cost. Magic scrolls cast a spell when used, at the cost of its reagent, and can fail depending on your Magic level. You can cast magic scrolls even while holding a weapon.

SHAPE SHIFTING
A mystical, somewhat rare and closely guarded skill that needs a lot of Wisdom to understand. It lets you take the form of many species, with their natural elemental damage and weaknesses. So far it only works with IIA's native species: Amphibious, Bloblic and Human practitioners can't take the forms of beasts from their old worlds. The Amphibious shared this ability with the Bloblic and Humans to secure their place among the High Races that now rule IIA.

HIDING
Turns you invisible to all beasts and players. As long as you stay still and don't attack, you stay invisible. The larger the species, the harder it is to hide. Reveal is the counter.

REVEAL
A purely player-versus-player skill. Used successfully, it casts a large area around you; any hidden player in it stops hiding and can be seen again. It's useful against enemy players who try to escape fights by hiding.

FAMILIARS
Spread across IIA are Scrollkeepers, entrusted by ancient mages with their precious scrolls. Once you find a scroll you can summon a rideable beast to travel IIA with. Familiars have no HP or weapons of their own; the rider attacks with their own weapon.

PILOT
Vehicles can be rewarded, bought or crafted, from jetskis to polycycles. Some have their own weapons; others let you use your own weapon while riding.

CRAFTING
Alchemy makes ammo bases, potions and rings, and gives a little Wisdom when crafting.
Engineering makes ammo, guns and jetpacks, and gives a little Dexterity when crafting.
Smithing makes armor, swords and vehicle parts, and gives a little Strength when crafting.
