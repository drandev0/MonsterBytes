---
name: Elements
category: GENERAL
---

From the game's in-game glossary. Every species on IIA resists each kind of damage by some amount; being strong against one element usually makes you weak against another. The eight elements, in the game's order: Normal, Fire, Water, Rune, Relic, Earth, Air, Death.

WEAKNESSES AT A GLANCE
Fire deals weakness damage to Death aligned species.
Water deals weakness damage to Fire aligned species.
Relic deals weakness damage to Water aligned species.
Earth deals weakness damage to Relic aligned species.
Air deals weakness damage to Earth aligned species.
Death deals no weakness damage to any species.

NORMAL
Normal is less of an element and more of the physical toughness of a species. Most species on IIA gain Normal resistance the higher their level. Most attacks deal dual damage per hit: one half Normal and the other half their chosen element. It's very rare for an attack to deal no Normal damage at all, so Normal resistance matters at every level. Magic specializes in dealing non-Normal damage, which makes mages dangerous to heavily armored species.
The hulking, tank-like Bloblic have an affinity for Normal thanks to their subdermal armor. The Amphibious have reduced Normal resistance because of their small size and twig-like limbs.
Pure Normal species can only reach 25% Fire resistance: armor easily becomes an oven when it's set alight. Certain massive enemies have extra Normal resistance. Air and Death aligned species have reduced resistance to Normal attacks.

FIRE
Fire is uncontrollable destruction, at least until it's doused by a bucket of Water. Fire aligned species are relatively uncommon: the element is so chaotic that it's hard for a species to use it naturally. Most Fire damage comes from crafted weapons or from magic.
The Amphibious have a natural affinity for Fire; it's difficult to incinerate a species with such slick, damp skin. The Bloblic have a natural weakness to Fire, because their bulky armor is built into their bodies.
Fire aligned species take weakness damage from Water attacks.

WATER
Abundant oceans dotted with islands make up much of IIA's surface, so Water aligned species are a common threat for travelers going from island to island. Relic is the most common and reliable way of dealing with IIA's aquatic species.
The Amphibious have a natural affinity for Water, as a semi-aquatic species that has long enjoyed the oceans and swamps of IIA.
Water aligned species have 0% Earth resistance and take weakness damage from Relic attacks.

RUNE
Rune is a special, barely understood element that exists only on IIA. Some think it is the life blood of the planet itself, seeping out of the core. What is understood is that Rune produces physical matter that can be interacted with. IIA's native species can consume it over time to gain incredible strength and change in appearance. The term "Rune Boss" comes from beasts that have consumed so much Rune that they have become almost an entirely new species: extremely powerful and larger than their kin.
That power comes at a cost: a Rune aligned species becomes very weak to one element (which one is not readable in our copy of the glossary). Pure Rune aligned species have heavily increased protection against most other elements.

RELIC
Relic is oddly named because of its history on IIA. After the High Races conquered IIA, strange relics older than the Shockions were unearthed, holding an unusual form of energy that looks like electricity. Since then every lightning or electricity attack falls under Relic.
Relic is the best choice for hunting Water aligned species; even semi-aquatic beings like the Amphibious are somewhat weak to it.
Relic aligned species take weakness damage from Earth attacks: grounding yourself is the best way to avoid frying on a live wire. Certain mechanized species have 0% Relic resistance.

EARTH
Earth is common on IIA. Most cave dwelling or land beasts have at least some Earth in them. Most species that embody it are rugged and bulky, though there are several nimble runners too. Ironically, many Earth species' last memory is of plummeting to the Earth after being dropped by an Air aligned species.
As the salt of the Earth, the Human Male has a mild affinity for this element; the Human Female has a mild weakness to it.
Earth aligned species have 0% Water resistance and take weakness damage from Air attacks.

AIR
Air superiority remains an unparalleled strength, even on IIA. Air is embodied in the flying beasts that hunt IIA's skies. In exchange for flight and speed, Air aligned species have hollow or light bones, which gives them unusually low Normal resistance: fragile to most weapons, but still very dangerous if they catch you unaware. An arrow to the wing is the fastest way to bring a flying beast down.
As the Valkyries of Trendor, the Human Female has a mild affinity for Air; the Human Male has a mild weakness to it.
Air aligned species have 0% Relic resistance.

DEATH
Death is a rare and powerful element. The only real defense against it is a hardy constitution. Most species on IIA have at least some resistance to it, but almost no native species can resist more than 25% of it. No species on IIA takes weakness damage from Death, but outside a species' own weakness Death will almost always do the most damage of any element. The downside is that only the most powerful magic or the rarest weapons can use it.
Death aligned species have reduced Normal resistance and take weakness damage from Fire attacks: the way to cure necrotized flesh is to cut it off and burn away the rot.
Because Death deals no weakness damage, the lowest Death resistance in normal circumstances is 0%. Almost any species aligned with a single element is a Pure species and has 0% Death resistance.
