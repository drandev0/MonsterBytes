## Steps

1. Talk to Samuan. Samuan is located on BM in a bunker on the west side kind of between the BM building and the Rig. He will spawn a Gonlon that you must kill. This gonlon is on top of one of the pillars south of Samuan.
2. Kill the Gonlon. Return to Samuan. He will advance you Pilot Skill to 2.00.
3. Talk to Samuan. He will spawn a Kidion Bat above the BM structure. The Kidion Bat will be the one that is stationary in the air.
4. Kill the Kidion Bat. Return to Samuan.
5. Talk to Samuan. He will give you your reward.

## Notes

You will have had to complete your race's Inceptor License quest to begin this one.
This quest is started by Samuan - Advance Inceptor Trainer, a Blob, on BM Island.
