## Steps

1. Talk to T-Forge. He will ask that you retrieve him 20 Dark Jouster Hearts, and 20 Mool stones.
2. Return to T-Forge when you have retrieved the item. He will spawn Toolpole, an AP, that you must kill.
3. Kill Toolpole and talk to T-Forge. He will send you to talk to his cousin, U-Forge outside BM on top of the entrance structure.
4. Talk to U-Forge. He will spawn a Kidion Lord, a Mad Kidion, that you must kill.
5. Return to U-Forge when you have killed the Kidion Lord. He will send you back to T-Forge.
6. Talk to T-Forge to receive your gun

## Notes

To begin this quest you must talk to T-Forge, a BL, in the Black Market.
