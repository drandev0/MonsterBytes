## Steps

1. Talk to Blapstion, a Rancon, located in the zone in room of the Catacombs. He will send you to talk to Nargas.
2. Talk to Nargas, an HM, located in the room you drop down into the catacombs. He will send you to find Vlas.
3. Talk to Vlas, a BL, located on Moon Rifts. He will spawn Salgon, an HM, for you to kill.
4. Kill Salgon, and return to Vlas.
5. Talk to Vlas, he will send you off to find Flogran.
6. Talk to Flogran, an AP, located in the Black Market Dungeon. He will spawn Algona, a BL, that you must kill.
7. Kill Algona, and return to Flogran, he will send you off to find Lasgat.
8. Talk to Lasgat, an HM, located in the Trendor Sewers. He will send you off to talk to Val.
9. Talk to Val, an HF, in the Catacombs. She will spawn Zeg, a BL, that you must kill.
10. Kill Zeg, and return to Val.
11. Talk to Val, she will spawn Jarude, a BL, that you must kill.
12. Kill Jarude, and return to Val.
13. Talk to Val, she will spawn herself Val, an HF, that you must kill.
14. Kill Val, and return to Val.
15. Talk to Val, she will send you back to Lasgat.
16. Talk to Lasgat, he will send you to talk to Tasgot.
17. Talk to Tasgot, an HM, located in the catacombs in the room with the Rancons. He will spawn Clospen, an HM, that you must kill.
18. Kill Clospen, and return to Tasgot.
19. Talk to Tasgot, he will ask you to retrieve 20 Eyezora Eyes.
20. Return to Tasgot when you have collected the 20 Eyezora Eyes. After talking to him, he will send you back to Flogran.
21. Talk to Flogran. He will send you back to Blasption.
22. Talk to Blasption, he will send you off to talk to Argos.
23. Talk to Argos, a Rancon, on Uoc Penn Peninsula. He will send you off to collect the Grey and Blue Runes.
24. Talk to Argos when you have retrieve the Grey and Blue Runes. He will spawn Glactian, a BL, that you must kill.
25. Kill Glactian, and return to Argos.
26. Talk to Argos, he will send you back to Flogran.
27. Talk to Flogran, he will send you off to find Kiston.
28. Talk to Kiston, an HM, located on top of the Trendor Towers, on top of one of the columns. He will send you to talk to Kiston's Servant.
29. Talk to Kiston's Servant, an HF, located on the beach between BC and the Root of the Oracle, she will spawn Overking Eidolon, a Zeth, that you must kill.
30. Kill Overking Eidolon, and return to Kiston's Servant.
31. Talk to Kiston's Servant, she will send you back to Kiston.
32. Talk to Kiston, he will spawn Los, a Mad Kidion, that you must kill.
33. Kill Los and return to Kiston.
34. Talk to Kiston. He will send you off to talk to Nostok.
35. Talk to Nostok, an HM, located in the Firetown safezone skull. He will spawn Crousha, an AP, that you must kill.
36. Kill Crousha, and return to Nostok.
37. Talk to Nostok, he will spawn Juspon, and HF, that you must kill.
38. Kill Juspon, and return to Nostok.
39. Talk to Nostok, he will spawn Uspec, and HM, that you must kill.
40. Kill Uspec, and return to Nostok.
41. Talk to Nostok, he will spawn Yast, a BL, that you must kill.
42. Kill Yast, and return to Nostok
43. Talk to Nostok, he will send you back to Kiston.
44. Talk to Kiston, he will send you back to Nostok.
45. Talk to Nostok, he will spawn Cas, an Eyezora that you must kill.
46. Kill Cas, and return to Nostok.
47. Talk to Nostok, he will spawn Wos, A High Priest of Cron, that you must kill.
48. Kill Wos, and return to Nostok.
49. Talk to Nostok, he will spawn Buton, a BL, that you must kill.
50. Kill Buton, and return to Nostok.
51. Talk to Nostok, he will send you back to Kiston.
52. Talk to Kiston, he will send you off to talk to Vecapan.
53. Talk to Vecapan, an Elite Shockion, located in the hidden room of the Catacombs behind the Rancons. He will send you to talk to Dablo.
54. Talk to Dablo, an Elder Shockion, located in the Black Market Dungeon. He will spawn Salspton, an Elite Shockion, that you must kill.
55. Kill Salspton, and return to Dablo.
56. Talk to Dablo, he will spawn Aquan, an Elite Shockion that you must kill.
57. Kill Aquan, and return to Dablo
58. Talk to Dablo, he will spawn Papstaon, a Shockion that you must kill.
59. Kill Papstaon, and return to Dablo
60. Talk to Dablo, he will spawn Nanontan, a Shockion that you must kill.
61. Kill Nanontan, and return to Dablo.
62. Talk to Dablo, he will spawn Bosia, a Rancon that you must kill.
63. Kill Bosia, and return to Dablo.
64. Talk to Dablo, he will spawn Pasp, an AP, that you must kill.
65. Kill Pasp, and return to Dablo.
66. Talk to Dalbo, he will send you back to Vecapan.
67. Talk to Vecapan, he will send you back to Blasption.
68. Talk to Blasption, he will send you off to spawn Tispton
69. Examine Tispton, a Rancon, located on Sial Island close to Ligs the Rigprion from the Level 11 armor quest. He will spawn himself since he is a Raid spawn.
70. Kill Tipston, and return to Blasption.
71. Talk to Blasption, he will spawn Cain, an HM, in the Catacombs in the Rancons room, that you must kill.
72. Kill Cain and return to Blasption. He will give you your emblem to access the Cave of ancients, and tell you to talk to Blasption's Servant, a Rigprion located right behind him for your armor reward.
73. Talk to Blasption's Servant for your armor reward.

## Notes

Need to have completed Level 12 Armor quest to begin.
Completing this quest will also give you access to the Cave of Ancients.
