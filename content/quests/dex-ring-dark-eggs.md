## Steps

1. Talk to Priadoom. He will ask you to retrieve his missing eggs from the BM Dungeon: Dark Ank Egg Dark Kaing Egg Dark Llias Egg Dark Ora Egg Dark Shoki Egg Dark Sia Egg Dark Toolip Egg Dark Umbra Egg Dark Xill Egg Dark Zi Egg
2. Collect all 10 Dark Eggs. Talk to Priadoom. He will give you your reward.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to Priadoom, a Godion, on the lower section of the Blark Market platform.
