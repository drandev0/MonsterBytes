## Steps

1. Talk to Lorton. He will ask you to buy a Scroll of Acid Missle.
2. Talk to Tamin (Magery), an AP, in the 4-Falls Magic Shop. Purchase a Scroll of Acid Missle from him. Return to Lorton.
3. Talk to Lorton. He will reward you with some Poison Herb. He will then ask you to go kill flobs looking for the Scroll of Weak Heal.
4. Go kill flobs until your retrieve the autoloot. Return to Lorton.
5. Talk to Lorton. He will reward you with some Bone. Return to Lorton when you are Level 2 Magic.
6. Talk to Lorton. He will tell you to talk to others to continue getting magic scrolls.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to Lorton, an AP, in the 4-Falls Magic Shop.
