## Steps

1. Talk to Uspoans on the western edge of MR. He will give you a list of items he requires: Rusted Item  (from Level 11 armor Quest) Black-market Field Report (from Level 11 armor Quest) Naila's Comb (From Naila magic quest) DigDig Root (plant extractor) GI Head (from Level 11 armor Quest) Ashadra Shell (mob in solothic caves)
2. Retrieve all the items. Then talk to Uspoans.
3. Talk to Trawoh to retrieve your reward

## Notes

This quest can be started once you have completed your Level 11 armor quest.
Each race recieves a Level 10 Gun upon completion of the hunt.
