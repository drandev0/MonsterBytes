## Steps

1. Talk to Rumula. Rumula is located on BC, by a tree near the bridge. He will ask you to retrieve 10 Warps Cells from the Engineering Shop in Trendor.
2. Once you return with your Warp Cells, Rumula will send you off to 4-Falls to retrieve the contents of 4 containers hidden by the waterfalls there.
3. Goto 4-Falls and find the 4 containers in order. Return to Rumula with the contents of the 4th container.
4. Talk to Rumula. He will advance your pilot skill to 3.00. He will then start you on Lesson 2, where you will be asked to locate Gonlons on HC and kill them.
5. Talk to Rumula he will spawn a Gonlon on the HC side of the HC-BC Bridge. Kill this Gonlon and locate Surgis, a Blob, located in the same area that the Gonlon spawned.
6. Talk to Surgis. He will spawn a Gonlon toward the South-Eastern side of HC, by a canopy. Kill this Gonlon and locate Bastic, a Blob, located in the same area that the Gonlon spawned.
7. Talk to Bastic. He will spawn a Gonlon toward the Western side of HC's Petrified Forest, just north of Destroyer01's rune crystal. Kill this Gonlon and locate Xeodux, a Blob, located in the same area that the Gonlon spawned.
8. Talk to Xeodux. He will spawn a Gonlon on the beach on the east coast of HC, by the Solothic Cave entrance. Kill this Gonlon and locate Iner, a Blob, located in the same area that the Gonlon spawned.
9. Talk to Iner. He will spawn a Gonlon on the beach on the northern coast of HC, by the Solothic Cave entrance. Kill this Gonlon and return to Rumula.
10. Talk to Rumula. He will give you your rewards: Pilot Skill to 3.25, 10 additional Warp Cells, and a Termine License.

## Notes

You will have to have your Advance Inceptor License to begin this quest.
This quest is started by Rumula, a Blob, on BC.
Upon completing this quest your pilot skill will be 3.25, enough to mount polycycles.
