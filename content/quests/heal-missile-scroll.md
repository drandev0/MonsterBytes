## Steps

1. Talk to Drodar, a HM, located just outside the AMC. He will ask you to kill Infant Diliriums until you retrieve their autoloot.
2. Kill Infant Diliriums until you get the Serpent Heal auto-loot, and return to Drodar.
3. Talk to Drodar. He then will ask you to retrieve a key from a Gope. Kill Gopes until you recieve the Drodar Key, then return to Drodar.
4. Talk to Drodar. He will send you into the AMC to find his chest that you must open with the key.
5. Find the chest, which is located in one of the back rooms of the AMC, and retrieve its contents: Drodar's Salve, and return to Drodar.
6. Talk to Drodar. He will send you off to kill an Elite Shockion.
7. Kill an Elite Shockion, and bring its head back to Drodar.
8. Talk to Drodar to recieve your scroll.

## Notes

This quest can be started once you are Level 3 in Magic.
