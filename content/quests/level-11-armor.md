## Steps

1. Talk to Gwenam, on the bridge, in the Alrilious Mining Cave
2. Talk to Glagrog, on the bridge, in the Alrilious Mining Cave. He will send you off to find his brother Ligs, on Sia Island.
3. Talk to Ligs, on Sia Island. He will spawn ISP, a Jouster, that you must kill.
4. Kill ISP, then talk to Ligs again. He will send you off to find his cousin Bis, in the Black Market.
5. Talk Bis, in the Black Market. He will spawn Listop, a Hidion, that you must kill
6. Kill Listop, then talk to Bis again. He will spawn Kro, a Slimdon, that you must kill
7. Kill Kro, then talk to Bis again. He will send you off to find Sol on Sluggith Island.
8. Talk to Sol on Sluggith Island. He will send you off to find Bok, in the AP Island Caves.
9. Talk to Bok, in the AP Island Caves. He will send you off to find Forgral, in the Black Market.
10. Talk to Forgral, in the Black Market. He will spawn Brisk, a HM, in the same area, by where you entered.
11. Kill Brisk, and talk to Forgral again. He will spawn Trin, a HF, at the opening of one of the tunnels.
12. Kill Trin, and talk to Forgral again. He will spawn Forgral, a BL, in a tunnel further into BM.
13. Kill Forgral, and talk to Forgral again. Return to Bok.
14. Talk to Bok. He will send you to talk to Phigon, in the AMC, under the bridge.
15. Talk to Phigon. He will spawn GI, a GI Bat, above the hill outside of AMC.
16. Kill GI, and then return to Phigon. The Gi bat, does not drop its head, it is given to you by Phigon. He will send you to talk to Glagrog.
17. Talk to Glagrog. He will send you to Safron, a HF, located on the same bridge, to collect your reward.
18. Talk to Safron. She will give you your reward.

## Notes

You will need your race leader's 9th gidion/badge/crest to begin this quest.
All NPCs for this quest are Rigprions.
Also when you have completed this quest you will have some items left over in your inventory. These items are used in the scavenger hunt #1 quest, so don't dispose of any of these items.
