## Steps

1. Talk to Fro, a Blob, at the beginning of the Solothic Caves by Trendor Bridge. He will spawn Sigrap, a Slimdon, in Death Valley.
2. Kill Sigrap. Return to Fro.
3. Talk to Fro's Servant, a White Godion, next to Fro for your reward.
