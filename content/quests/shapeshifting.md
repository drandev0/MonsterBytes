## Steps

1. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Flob [L1] Return to Sial once you have Level 1 SS (basically just use the orb once, and you will be level 1)
2. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to find Laetion, a Flob, located around the bridge area of Trendor.
3. Talk to Laetion. He will give you a letter to give to Sial. Return to Sial.
4. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Infant Stount [L2]. Return to Sial when you are Level 2 SS.
5. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to find Plispon, an Infant Stount, located right next to the skeleton in Death Valley.
6. Talk to Plispon. He will send you off to find his brother Cross, a Farion, located outside of the AMC.
7. Talk to Cross. He will send you back to Sial. Return to Sial
8. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Klondon [L3]. Return to Sial when you are Level 3 SS.
9. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to find Slaps, a Klondon, located on foothill by the water outside of the Bloblic Colonies.
10. Talk to Slaps. He will send you off to find Loat, a Klondon, located on the hills to the West of AMC.
11. Talk to Loat. He will send you back to Slaps.
12. Talk to Slaps. He will send you back to Sial.
13. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Ean [L4]. Return to Sial when you are Level 4 SS.
14. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to find Klasp, an Ean, by the DV skeleton, but more toward the eastern hills.
15. Talk to Klasp. He will send you off to gather some supplies for him: Neglik Stinger (5) Tug Seed (10) Zogs Health Elixir (5) Gurg Seed (5)
16. Talk to Klasp once you have gathered the required items. He will send you off to find his brother, Saspla, an Ean, located on the eastern shores of Sial Island
17. Talk to Saspla. He will spawn a Mutated Trisk that you must kill.
18. Kill the Mutated Trisk and return to Saspla.
19. Talk to Saspla. He will spawn a Mutated Crid, that you must kill.
20. Kill the Mutated Crid, and return to Saspla.
21. Talk to Saspla. He will send you back to Sial
22. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Ligon [L5]. Return to Sial when you are Level 5 SS.
23. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to find Sush, a Fadun, located in the park at the bottom of Trendor.
24. Talk to Sush. She will send you off to find here sister Bislion, a Ligon, located in the Solothic Caves.
25. Talk to Bislion. She will spawn a Mutated Shockion in the same cave that you must kill.
26. Kill the Mutated Shockion and return to Bislion.
27. Talk to Bislion. She will send you back to Sush.
28. Talk to Sush. She will send you back to Sial.
29. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Stontrile [L6]. Return to Sial when you are Level 6 SS.
30. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to kill a Mutated Stontrile. The spawn is close to the shore, and Trendor Tower will be off in the distance.
31. Kill the Mutated Stontrile and return to Sial.
32. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Shrite [L7]. Return to Sial when you are Level 7 SS, and have become Champion of IIA, meaning you have the Championship Belt of Savtan.
33. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Ascherion [L8], and send you off to find Vor, an Ascherion, on Unity.
34. Talk to Vor. Vor will be located on the western side of Unity, close to the shore/land intersection. He will spawn a Mutated Ascherion that you must kill.
35. Kill the Mutated Ascherion and return to Vor.
36. Talk to Vor. He will send you back to Sial.
37. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Adult Shockion [L9]. Return to Sial when you are Level 9 SS.
38. Talk to Sial. He will give your next orb, and send you off to find Cleasp, an Elite Shockion, on Black Market.
39. Talk to Cleasp. Cleasp will be located on the western side of Black Market, close to the shore where Blin from the Championship Belt was located. He will spawn a Mutated Elite Shockion that you must kill.
40. Kill the Mutated Elite Shockion and return to Cleasp.
41. Talk to Cleasp. He will send you back to Sial.
42. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Pidion [L10]. Return to Sial when you are Level 10 SS.
43. Talk to Sial. He will spawn a Delusional Pos, a Vigon, by the Sial Comm Tower.
44. Kill the Delusional Pos and return to Sial.
45. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Lauder [L11]. Return to Sial when you are Level 11 SS.
46. Talk to Sial. He will send you off to find Zagison, a Lauder, in the Cave of Ancients.
47. Talk to Zagison, located in the center of the plant room #1. He will ask you to retrieve the following items: 6 High Priest Plasmas 6 Eyzora Eyes 1 Earth Rune 1 Air Rune 1 Water Rune 1 Fire Rune
48. Talk to Zagison once you have retrieved his items. He will spawn himself, a Lauder, that you must kill.
49. Kill Zagison and return to Zagison.
50. Talk to Zagison, he will send you back to Sial.
51. Talk to Sial. He will give you the Orb of Agron [L12]. Return to Sial when you are Level 12 SS.
52. Talk to Sial. He will inform you that there are no more Orbs to quest.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to Sial, an AP, on Sial Island to start the quest.
NOTE: After your initial conversation with Sial, always talk to him ShapeShifted, otherwise he will start you over from the beginning. Also whenever talking to a NPC during the quest make sure you ShapeShift into their form.
To find Sial, start on top of Trendor Tower, and look west toward Sial Island, you should see 3 peaks: 1 high, 1 low, and 1 high. Sial is on that lower (middle) peak, on the southern side of it.
