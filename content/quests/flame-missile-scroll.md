## Steps

1. Talk to Naila, a HF, located just outside the Solothic Cave opening by the Trendor Bridge. She will ask you to retrieve 10 Tanzin Bushes for her.
2. Retrieve the 10 Tanzin Bushes, which can be bought in a magic shop, and return to Naila.
3. Talk to Naila. She then will ask you to retrieve a key from an Adult Dilirium. Kill Adult Diliriums until you recieve the Naila Key, then return to Naila.
4. Talk to Naila. She will send you into the Solothic Caves to find her chest that you must open with the key.
5. Find the chest, which is located in the room with the Didons, and retrieve its contents: Naila's Comb, and return to Naila.
6. Talk to Naila. She will send you off to retrieve 3 Taldon eyes.
7. Kill 3 Taldons, and bring their eyes back to Naila.
8. Talk to Naila to recieve your scroll.

## Notes

This quest can be started once you are Level 3 in Magic.
