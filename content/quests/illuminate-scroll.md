## Steps

1. Talk to Detinu, a HM, located just outside the Crypts in 4-Falls. He will ask you to kill Vigons until you retrieve their autoloot.
2. Kill Vigons until you get 5 Perox Flowers auto-looted, and return to Detinu.
3. Talk to Detinu. He then will ask you to retrieve a key from a Kidion Bat. Kill Kidion Bats until you recieve the Detinu Key, then return to Detinu.
4. Talk to Detinu. He will send you into the Crypts to find his chest that you must open with the key.
5. Find the chest, which is located at the end of the tunnel before dropping down into the actual crypt (same room as the Crids), and retrieve its contents: Detinu's Flint, and return to Detinu.
6. Talk to Detinu. He will send you off to kill a Mayzora.
7. Kill a Mayzora, and bring its spine back to Detinu.
8. Talk to Detinu to recieve your scroll.

## Notes

This quest can be started once you are Level 3 in Magic.
