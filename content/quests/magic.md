## Steps

1. Talk to Lorton. He will ask you to buy a Scroll of Acid Missle. STEP_NEED STEP_GIVES
2. Talk to Tamin (Magery), an AP, in the 4-Falls Magic Shop. Purchase a Scroll of Acid Missle from him. Return to Lorton. STEP_NEED STEP_GIVES Scroll of Acid Missile,1
3. Talk to Lorton. He will reward you with some Poison Herb. He will then ask you to go kill flobs looking for the Scroll of Weak Heal. STEP_NEED Scroll of Acid Missile,1 STEP_GIVES Poison Herb,12
4. Go kill flobs until your retrieve the autoloot. Return to Lorton. STEP_NEED STEP_GIVES Scroll of Weak Heal,1
5. Talk to Lorton. He will reward you with some Bone. Return to Lorton when you are Level 2 Magic. STEP_NEED Scroll of Weak Heal,1 STEP_GIVES Bone,6
6. Talk to Lorton. STEP_NEED * Level 2 Magic,1 STEP_GIVES

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to Lorton, an AP, in the 4-Falls Magic Shop.
