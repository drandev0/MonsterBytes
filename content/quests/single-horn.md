## Steps

1. Talk to Shaman Raphic to begin the quest. He will give you keys to open chest in the Crypts.
2. Enter the Crypt to retrieve the minerals needed for Shaman Raphic. The Chalcedony chest is located on the floor of the Mad Kidion room. The Aventurine chest is located in the back right of the top floor. Chalcedony and Aventurine also rare drop off of mobs if you would rather avoid the Crypt. Crosps should be roaming the area around the main city of 4-Falls.

## Notes

This quest requires that your Mind Control is at least 6.
Talk to Shaman Raphic, an AP, located in the mushroom village of 4-Falls.
There are a variety of Single Horns that you may receive, it is random which one you will be given when you have completed the quest.
