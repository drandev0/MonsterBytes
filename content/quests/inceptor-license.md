## Steps

1. Talk to Draken. Draken is located behind the store on AP Island, out on one of the line strips of land that extend from the main island. He will send you out to find the Lesson 1 Canister.
2. Find and open the Lession 1 Canister. The canister is located on one of the leafs behind the AP Store. Return to Draken.
3. Talk to Draken. He will send you out to find the Lesson 2 Canister.
4. Find and open the Lession 2 Canister. The canister is located on one of the trees. Return to Draken.
5. Talk to Draken. He will give you your reward. You will also raise your Pilot Skill to 1.50

## Notes

This quest is started by Draken - Amphibious Master of Flight.
