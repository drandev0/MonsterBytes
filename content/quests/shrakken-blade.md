## Steps

1. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Shockion , that you must kill
2. Kill the Insane Shockion, then return to Ghos Kalin.
3. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Jouster , that you must kill
4. Kill the Insane Jouster, then return to Ghos Kalin.
5. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Trisk , that you must kill
6. Kill the Insane Trisk, then return to Ghos Kalin.
7. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Elite Ascherion , that you must kill
8. Kill the Insane Elite Ascherion, then return to Ghos Kalin.
9. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Deathbot , that you must kill
10. Kill the Insane Deathbot, then return to Ghos Kalin.
11. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Agron , that you must kill
12. Kill the Insane Agron, then return to Ghos Kalin.
13. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will spawn an Insane Skarshar , that you must kill
14. Kill the Insane Skarshar, then return to Ghos Kalin.
15. Talk to Ghos Kalin. He will give you a key to the shrakken chest located in the stone maze in BM.
16. Open the chest in the Stone Maze for your reward.

## Notes

To begin this quest you must talk to Ghos Kalin, a BL, on the HC side of the HC-to-BC Bridge.
