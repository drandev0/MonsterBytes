## Steps

1. Talk to Vigoss, a Vigon, by Trendor City.
2. Talk to Vigoss, he will send you off to kill flobs.
3. Kill flobs until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Vigoss.
4. Talk to Vigoss. He will spawn Grak, a flob, that you must kill.
5. Kill Grak, and return to Vigoss. He will send you off to find the Infant Stount Champion.
6. Talk to Sacoi, an Infant Stount, located in the Solothic Caves. He will send you off to kill Infant Stounts.
7. Kill Infant Stounts until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Sacoi.
8. Talk to Sacoi. He will spawn Lybyan, an Infant Stount, that you must kill.
9. Kill Lybyan, and return to Sacoi. He will send you off to find the Clegurk Champion.
10. Talk to QI, a Clegurk, located on the Rig side of Black Market Island. He will send you off to kill Clegurks.
11. Kill Clegurks until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to QI.
12. Talk to QI. He will spawn Misci, a Clegurk, that you must kill.
13. Kill Misci, and return to QI. He will send you off to find the Infant Dilirium Champion.
14. Talk to Ablis, an Infant Dilirium, located in the Solothic Caves. He will send you off to kill Infant Diliriums.
15. Kill Infant Diliriums until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Ablis.
16. Talk to Ablis. He will spawn Blag, an Infant Dilirium, that you must kill.
17. Kill Blag, and return to Ablis. He will send you off to find the Fadun Champion.
18. Talk to Varon, a Fadun, located on Black Market Island. He will send you off to kill Faduns.
19. Kill Faduns until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Varon.
20. Talk to Varon. He will spawn Nia, a Fadun, that you must kill.
21. Kill Nia, and return to Varon. He will send you off to find the Klondon Champion.
22. Talk to Dapcen, a Klondon, located on Human City between Trendor Tower and Death Valley. He will send you off to kill Klondons.
23. Kill Klondons until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Dapcen.
24. Talk to Dapcen. He will spawn Lacran, a Klondon, that you must kill.
25. Kill Lacran, and return to Dapcen. He will send you off to find the Ean Champion.
26. Talk to Fisiel, an Ean, located near the Bloblic Colonies. He will send you off to kill Eans.
27. Kill Eans until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Fisiel.
28. Talk to Fisiel. He will spawn Psykian, an Ean, that you must kill.
29. Kill Psykian, and return to Fisiel. He will send you off to find the Adult Dilirium Champion.
30. Talk to Oian, an Adult Dilirium, located on the southern edge of Moon Rifts. He will send you off to kill Adult Diliriums.
31. Kill Adult Diliriums until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Oian.
32. Talk to Oian. He will spawn Yi, an Adult Dilirium, that you must kill.
33. Kill Yi, and return to Oian. He will send you off to find the Stontrile Champion.
34. Talk to Blin, a Stontrile, located near the shores of Black Market (Western Side by Claesp). He will send you off to kill Stontriles.
35. Kill Stontriles until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Blin.
36. Talk to Blin. He will spawn Yial, a Stontrile, that you must kill.
37. Kill Yial, and return to Blin. He will send you off to find the Naton Champion.
38. Talk to Rocon, a Naton, located on Moon Rifts by the stone huts. He will send you off to kill Natons.
39. Kill Natons until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Rocon.
40. Talk to Rocon. He will spawn Niz, a Naton, that you must kill.
41. Kill Niz, and return to Rocon. He will send you off to find the Didon Champion.
42. Talk to Tennia, a Gonlon, located on the Inside Black Market, Human Side. He will send you off to kill Didons.
43. Kill Didons until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Tennia.
44. Talk to Tennia. He will spawn Ziffian, a Didon, that you must kill.
45. Kill Ziffian, and return to Tennia. He will send you off to find the Neglik Champion.
46. Talk to Papcaca, a Neglik, located just outside the AMC. He will send you off to kill Negliks.
47. Kill Negliks until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Papcaca.
48. Talk to Papcaca. He will spawn P, a Neglik, that you must kill.
49. Kill P, and return to Papcaca. He will send you off to find the Sihopin Champion.
50. Talk to Gaspial, a Sihopin, located on the Island between BC and Root of the Oracle. Sihopin are yellow sparkles, and are kinda hard to see. Gaspiel is located by a tree and toward the middle of the island. Gaspial wastes no time having you kill other Sihopins, instead he will spawn Jel, a Sihopin, that you must kill.
51. Kill Jel, and return to Gaspiel. He will send you off to find the Stount Champion.
52. Talk to Hilip, a Stount, located on Unity. He will send you off to kill Stounts.
53. Kill Stounts until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Hilip.
54. Talk to Hilip. He will spawn Via, a Stount, that you must kill.
55. Kill Via, and return to Hilip. He will send you off to find the Trine Champion. He will also give you the Agron's Spirit Boost, which can be equipped when you are Shapeshifted into an Agron.
56. Talk to Laspcan, an Eyemba, located in the water between the rig and the BM Comm Tower. He will send you off to kill Trines.
57. Kill Trines until you retrieve the autoloot. Return to Laspcan.
58. Talk to Laspcan. He will spawn Savtan, a Zorphion-clone, that you must kill.
59. Kill Savtan, and return to Laspcan. This will end the Championship Belt for IIA. You will also receive the Trine Aqua Force, which can be equipped when you are shapeshifted into a Trine.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
