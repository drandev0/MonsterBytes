## Steps

1. Talk to G'fron. He will ask you to retrieve his missing eggs: Dominion Briging Egg Dominion Briscore Egg Dominion Cripton Egg Dominion Drout Egg Dominion Flisk Egg Dominion Gratute Egg Dominion Grison Egg Dominion Ligton Egg Dominion Powlong Egg
2. Collect all 9 Dominion Eggs. Talk to G'fron. He will give you your reward.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to G-fron, a Godion, in the Trendor Dance Club.
