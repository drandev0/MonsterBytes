## Steps

1. Talk to Talicor. He will ask you to retrieve 10 each of the following minerals: Talpite Golan Crystals Varlenite Orgate Azagate Fripe Shale
2. Talk to Talicor after you have retrieved all the minerals from the last step. He will ask you to retrieve 20 each of the follwing minerals: Odion Gem Evangosilt Puron Lafe Stone Zin Clad Wisnox Orthonite
3. Talk to Talicor after you have retrieved all the minerals from the last step. He will ask you to retrieve 30 each of the following minerals: Mool Stone Quartz Lyzantate Naggite Rocap Ore Zancon
4. Talk to Talicor after you have retrieved all the minerals from the last step. He will ask you to retrieve a final set of items: Elder Meeting Stone Water Stone (5) Blue Stone Rune
5. Talk to Talicor after you have retrieved all the items from the last step. He will give you the Lava Stone Extractor.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to Talicor, a BL, in the Blobic Colonies.
Talicor is located in the Lobby.
