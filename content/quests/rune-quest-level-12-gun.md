## Steps

1. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will give you a Amulet of Rune Protection. They will send you to kill Lord Shraderix.
2. Lord Shraderix is located in the Black Market Dungeon. Enter the dungeon, take the first tunnel left, this will lead you to Lord Shraderix. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
3. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Shipron.
4. Lord Shipron's Crystal is located on Sluggith Island. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
5. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Frube.
6. Frube's Crystal is located on the Root of the Oracle. Kill her and return to your Rune NPC.
7. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Zorphion.
8. Zorphion's Crystal is located by the Trisk Hive. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
9. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Skelcy.
10. Skelcy's Crystal is located on Black Market Island. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
11. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Destroyer01.
12. Destroyer01's Crystal is located in the Petrified Forest. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
13. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Slate.
14. Slate's Crystal is located on Sia Island. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
15. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Brike.
16. Brike's Crystal is located on Sia Island. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
17. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Zeth.
18. Zeth's Crystal is located on Unity. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
19. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will send you to kill Pridion.
20. Pridion's Crystal is located on Moon's Rift. Kill him and return to your Rune NPC.
21. Talk to your Rune NPC. They will give you your reward. AP = HF = HM = BL =

## Notes

This quest can be started once you are level 8 or higher.
Each race has a different Rune NPC to interact with:
AP = Strod
BL = Crod
HF = Emril
HM = Zarwen
NOTE: always wear your Amulet of Rune Protection when fighting the Runebosses.
