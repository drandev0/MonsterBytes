## Steps

1. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Shockion on the beach, that you must kill
2. Kill the Insane Shockion, then return to Swami B'll.
3. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Jouster on the beach, that you must kill
4. Kill the Insane Jouster, then return to Swami B'll.
5. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Trisk on the beach, that you must kill
6. Kill the Insane Trisk, then return to Swami B'll.
7. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Elite Ascherion on the beach, that you must kill
8. Kill the Insane Elite Ascherion, then return to Swami B'll.
9. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Deathbot on the beach, that you must kill
10. Kill the Insane Deathbot, then return to Swami B'll.
11. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Agron on the beach, that you must kill
12. Kill the Insane Agron, then return to Swami B'll.
13. Talk to Swami B'll. He will spawn an Insane Skarshar on the beach, that you must kill
14. Kill the Insane Skarshar, then return to Swami B'll.
15. Talk to Swami B'll. He will give you a key to the trinster chest located in the back left room of the AMC.
16. Open the chest in the AMC for your reward.

## Notes

To begin this quest you must talk to Swami B'll, an AP, on beach between Sluggith Island and AP Island.
You also must have completed the [AP] Stontrile quest to begin.
