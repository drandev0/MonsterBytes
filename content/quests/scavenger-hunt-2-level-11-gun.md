## Steps

1. Talk to Hrestonas on the western edge of MR. He will give you a list of items he requires: Championship Belt of Grak (Flob championship belt) Blue Jewel (from Level 12 Armor quest) Rocap Ore (mineral extractor) Broken Hammer (from Level 12 Armor quest) Dark Zi Egg (in BM Dungeon) Detinu's Flint (from Detinu's Magic quest) Living Rune (lord shaderix, mob in BM dungeons)
2. Retrieve all the items. Then talk to Hrestonas. He will give you an additional 25000 in credits if you also have the Gisgis Axe from the Level 12 armor quest in your inventory.
3. Talk to Mooggle to retrieve your reward.

## Notes

This quest can be started once you have completed your Level 12 armor quest.
Each race recieves a Level 11 Gun upon completion of the hunt.
