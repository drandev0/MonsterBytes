## Steps

1. Talk to Ocsylop, a Sarin, in Firetown. Be sure to have your Glagrog Key in your inventory. He will send you off to find Trus.
2. Talk to Trus, a sarin, is located on Unity. He will spawn Usag, a sarin, that you must kill.
3. Kill Usag, and return to Trus.
4. Talk to Trus. He will send you off to find Klion, a sarin, inside the BM dungeon.
5. Talk to Klion. He will spawn Zolog, a sarin, that you must kill.
6. Kill Zolog, and return to Klion.
7. Talk to Klion. He will spawn Brias, a sarin, that you must kill.
8. Kill Brias, and return to Klion.
9. Talk to Klion. He will spawn Ligadoom, a blob, that you must kill.
10. Kill Ligadoom, and return to Klion.
11. Talk to Klion. He will spawn Sospoon, an elite shockion, that you must kill.
12. Kill Sospoon, and return to Klion.
13. Talk to Klion. He will spawn Nillin, a sarin, that you must kill.
14. Kill Nillin, and return to Klion.
15. Talk to Klion. He will send you off to find Requess, a sarin, in the solothic caves.
16. Talk to Requess. He will spawn Purt, a sarin in Death Valley.
17. Kill Purt. Return to Requess.
18. Talk to Requess. He will send you off to find Quis, a sarin, on the western coast of Sial Island.
19. Talk to Quis. He will send you off to find Digson, a sarin, in the AMC.
20. Talk to Digson. He will spawn Acon, a Dark Jouster, that you must kill.
21. Kill Acon, and return to Digson.
22. Talk to Digson. He will spawn Bosdon, a Crid, that you must kill.
23. Kill Bosdon, and return to Digson.
24. Talk to Digson. He will spawn Lolo, a Deathbot, that you must kill.
25. Kill Lolo, and return to Digson.
26. Talk to Digson. He will send you off to find Ylog, a sarin, located under the bridge to Bloblic City.
27. Talk to Ylog, he will spawn Gis, a sarin, tha you must kill
28. Kill Gis, and return to Ylog.
29. Talk to Ylog. He will send you off to find Grospon, a sarin, in Firetown.
30. Talk to Grospon. He will spawn Glolo, an Elder Shockion, that you must kill.
31. Kill Glolo, and return to Grospon.
32. Talk to Grospon. He will send you to talk to Ocsylop
33. Talk to Ocsylop. He will send you to talk to Ocsylop's Servant for your reward.
34. Talk to Ocsylop's Servant for your reward.

## Notes

Need to have completed Level 11 Armor quest to begin.
All NPCs for this quest are Sarins.
Also when you complete this quest there will left over items in your inventory. Don't discard of these items, as they are used in the scavenger hunt #2 quest.
