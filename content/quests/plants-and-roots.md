## Steps

1. Talk to Srag. He will ask you to retrieve 5 each of the following plants: Tug Seed Gurg Seed Asgon Plant Mandion Shell Docux Bean Frodigon Sap
2. Talk to Srag after you have retrieved all the plants from the last step. He will ask you to retrieve 10 each of the following plants: Inseraitus Bark Wigid Nut Hig Fern Viastic Stem Sigpruce Edipidone Pollen Filidian Syrup Buctun Weed
3. Talk to Srag after you have retrieved all the plants from the last step. He will ask you to retrieve 20 each of the following plants: Sagelmic Bulb Ecolalgae Magus Leaf Digdig Root Wigspruce Stalk Acban Bean
4. Talk to Srag after you have retrieved all the plants from the last step. He will give you the final extractor.

## Notes

This quest can be started at any time.
Talk to Srag, an AP, on AP Island.
Srag is located at the beginning of the AP Island Cave.
