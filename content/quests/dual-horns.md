## Steps

1. Talk to Aner to begin the quest.
2. Enter the Crypt to retrieve the minerals needed for Aner. The mineral chest is located in the Skarshar Pit of the Crypt. The minerals can also be extracted from various locations in IIA, if you wish to avoid the Crypt.
3. Kill 2 Crosps for Aner. Crosps should be roaming the area around the main city of 4-Falls.
4. Return to Shaman Raphic to obtain your reward.

## Notes

This quest requires that you have completed the Single Horn Quest.
Talk to Aner, an AP, located in the Weapons Shop of 4-Falls.
There are a variety of Dual Horns you can get, and it is random which one you will receive upon completing the quest.
