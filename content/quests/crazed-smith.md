## Steps

1. Talk to Conzure to begin the quest.
2. Retrieve the items he needs: 5 Golan Crystals 5 Evangosilt 5 Naggite 2 Andros Alloy Units-SMT 10 Dark Jouster Hearts
3. Talk to Conzure to retrieve your sword
4. Repeat as often as yo wish to collect all the swords.

## Notes

Talk to Conzure - Crazed Smith, a Blob, located in the Black Market, in the same tunnel as the Dark Ora Egg.
There are a variety of swords you can get, and it is random which one you will receive.
