## Steps

1. Quest 1: Kill 10 Flobs
2. Quest 2: Kill 10 Clegurks
3. Quest 3: Kill 10 Infant Stounts
4. Quest 4: Kill 10 Vigons
5. Quest 5: Kill 10 Faduns Kill 10 Didons
6. Quest 6: Kill 10 Stontriles Kill 10 Malia Bats
7. Quest 7: Kill 10 Ascherions
8. Quest 8: Kill 10 Shockions Kill 10 Adult Shockions
9. Quest 9: Kill 10 Elite Shockions Kill 5 Elder Shockions

## Notes

Each race has its own race leader. And the first 9 quests are the same for all races. The 10-16 quests are different per race.
Talk to your race leader to begin these quests:
AP = Strife (located outside the shop on AP Island)
BL = Bo (located in the Lobby of the BC complex)
HM = Darius (located in the Karma Club, top floor of Trendor)
HF = Celeste (located on the bottom floor of Trendor)
Arming the Gidions/Badges/Crests will increase your STR and DEX, but also hinders the gaining of these stats while armed.
